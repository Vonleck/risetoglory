STORE LISTING (ENGLISH)

App name: Rise to Glory
Developer name: Aapo Virola
Category: Game > Casual (or Simulation)

SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 characters or fewer)
Rise from peasant to god-emperor in this medieval idle clicker.

FULL DESCRIPTION
Start as a penniless peasant mucking out stables and rise through 25 ranks to become a God-Emperor.

Tap to earn coins, learn skills, build an empire and let your fortune grow even while you are away.

FEATURES
- 25 ranks, from Peasant and Stable Hand to King, Emperor and God-Emperor
- Dozens of jobs and skills you can learn and upgrade, from begging at the gate to court intrigue
- Buy houses, horses and lordships as investments. They wear down and cost upkeep, so keep them in good condition or sell them
- Hire servants and take a spouse for bonuses, but watch the wages
- Once you are a king, found cities, rule subjects and set your taxes
- Collect 32 relics in four rarities from chests, each with its own artwork
- Try your luck in the back alley with coin flips, dice, roulette and slots, using virtual coins only
- Dig for gems in the gem mine, complete achievements and earn honorary titles
- Be reborn for permanent bonuses
- Earn coins while you are offline
- Ambient medieval music and a parchment-and-scroll look
- Works offline, no account needed, no ads

RISE TO GLORY PRO (one-time purchase)
Unlock more skills, estates, lordships and cities, extra gambling games, rebirth, longer offline earnings and a gem bonus.

The game contains optional in-app purchases and simulated gambling with virtual coins only. No real money can be won or lost.
